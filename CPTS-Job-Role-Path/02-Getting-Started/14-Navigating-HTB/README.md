# Navigating HTB

## Overview

A quick tour of the Hack The Box platform itself — the profile and rankings pages, and each section under the Labs tab (Tracks, Machines, Challenges, Fortress, Endgame, Pro Lab) along with Battlegrounds. Knowing the layout before diving in avoids wasted time hunting for features mid-engagement.

## Learning Objectives

- Navigate the profile and rankings pages
- Understand each section of the Labs tab
- Differentiate Active vs. Retired machines and their ranking implications
- Understand rank requirements for Fortress and Endgame
- Understand the Pro Lab lineup and Battlegrounds match limits

## Profile and Rankings

The **profile page** shows rank, progress, stats, badges/certificates earned, and a shareable profile link. The **rankings page** covers user, team, university, and country rankings, plus a separate VIP ranking track.

## Tracks

Curated selections of machines and challenges built around mastering a specific subject — created by HTB itself, partner companies, universities, or the community.

## Machines

Two machines are always featured: the latest weekly release and a staff pick.

| Type | Count | Ranking | Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active | Always 20 live at once | Counts toward ranking | Must be solved blind (no public walkthrough) |
| Retired | Rotates in as machines retire | Counts toward VIP ranking only | Official/community walkthroughs exist; requires VIP (except the 2 most recently retired) |

Both lists are filterable and sortable by difficulty, OS, and completion status.

## Challenges

Similar layout to machines: 10 categories, a maximum of 10 challenges per category, with separate Active and Retired tabs.

## Fortress

Vulnerable labs built by external companies, each with several flags to capture and a badge on completion. Some Fortresses are linked to job offers from the sponsoring company. Requires **Hacker rank or above** to access.

## Endgame

Multi-machine virtual labs simulating a real pentest scenario end-to-end. Active Endgames require **Guru rank**; Retired Endgames are available to any rank with a VIP subscription.

## Pro Labs

Large simulated enterprise networks requiring a separate subscription, with a certificate of completion awarded at the end:

| Pro Lab | Focus |
|---|---|
| Dante | Beginner-friendly introduction |
| Offshore | Active Directory corporate network |
| Cybernetics | Hardened AD, advanced difficulty |
| RastaLabs | Red team simulation |
| APTLabs | Most advanced — MSP-targeted attack simulation |

## Battlegrounds

Real-time competitive game modes:

- **Cyber Mayhem** — team of 4, attack/defense
- **Server Siege** — team of 2, attack-only speed race

Match limits: 2/month on free accounts, 5/month on VIP, 10/month on VIP+.

## Skills Practiced

- Platform navigation and feature discovery
- Understanding rank-gated content so time isn't wasted chasing inaccessible labs

## Key Takeaways

- Active machines must be solved blind — no walkthroughs exist yet, which is intentional and part of what counts toward live ranking.
- Rank requirements (Hacker for Fortress, Guru for Active Endgame) mean the platform expects a progression — don't expect access to everything on day one.
- Pro Labs are a distinct subscription and commitment from the regular machine/challenge pool — treat them as a milestone, not a starting point.
