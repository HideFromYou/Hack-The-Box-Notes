# Footprinting Lab — Easy

## Status

**Status: Completed**

## Lab notes

The Easy practical lab was completed as part of the HTB Academy Footprinting module.

The exercise applied the module's service-enumeration workflow: identify exposed services, investigate the information each service makes available, and correlate findings.

## Methodology

1. Perform service discovery and version detection.
2. Enumerate the exposed services with protocol-appropriate tools.
3. Validate interesting findings manually.
4. Record the evidence and the reasoning that led to each next step.
